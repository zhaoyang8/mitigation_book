2.2. Distorted density for climate models #

The decision-maker has a baseline probability \(dP_t(\theta)\) for each time instant, \(t\), and makes an adjustment for ambiguity by solving

\[\begin{split} \begin{aligned} \min_{q,\;\int_\Theta q(\theta)\,dP_t(\theta)=1}\; &\;\chi \int_\Theta q(\theta)\,\log q(\theta)\,dP_t(\theta) \\ \text{s.t. }\;& \int_\Theta \mu\!\bigl[X_t,\Phi^{\ast}(X_t)\bigr]\, q(\theta)\,dP_t(\theta)=\bar h(X_t), \end{aligned} \end{split}\]

where \(\Phi^{\ast}\) is the robustly optimal solution and \(\bar h\) is deduced from a preference specification with misspecification aversion. The minimizing density takes the form of

\[ q_t^{\ast}(\theta) \;\propto\; \exp\!\Bigl[-\zeta\,\mu\!\bigl(X_t,a^{\ast}(X_t)\bigr)\Bigr], \qquad \text{with }\zeta\text{ chosen so the constraint holds}. \]

2.3. Distorted density for the time of the first jump #

By conditioning first on the state-variable history, we find that the probability that a jump has occurred by time \(t\) is

\[ F_t \;=\; 1 - \exp\!\Bigl[ - \sum_{\ell=1}^{L} \int_{0}^{t} \mathcal J^{\ell}\!\bigl(X_\tau\bigr)\, d\tau \Bigr], \]

which increases in \(t\).
The implied density obtained by differentiating with respect to \(t\) is

\[ f_t \;=\; \sum_{\ell=1}^{L} \mathcal J^{\ell}(X_t)\, \exp\!\Bigl[ - \int_{0}^{t} \mathcal J^{\ell}\!\bigl(X_\tau\bigr)\, d\tau \Bigr]. \]

We express \(f\) as

\[ f_t \;=\; \sum_{\ell=1}^{L} f^{\ell}(t), \]

where

\[ f_t^{\ell} \overset{\mathrm{def}}{=} \mathcal J^{\ell}(X_t)\, \exp\!\Bigl[ - \sum_{\ell=1}^{L} \int_{0}^{t} \mathcal J^{\ell}\!\bigl(X_\tau\bigr)\, d\tau \Bigr]. \]
